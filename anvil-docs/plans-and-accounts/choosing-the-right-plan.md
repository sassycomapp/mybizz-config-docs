# [Choosing The Right Plan](#choosing-the-right-plan)

We offer four different price plans, each designed to cater to different types of developers and their specific needs. This document is a high level explanation of the plans set out in pricing page. It explains what each plan is for and what use cases they’re aimed at.

## [Free Plan](#free-plan)

-   **Who it’s for**: Beginners, hobbyists, or anyone who wants to try out Anvil without any financial commitment.
-   **Features and Benefits**:
    -   Basic features sufficient for learning and experimenting.
    -   Limited resources, such as reduced storage and limited app usage.
    -   Ideal for educational purposes, small personal projects, or just getting a feel for the platform.

## [Hobby Plan](#hobby-plan)

-   **Who it’s for**: Individual developers, enthusiasts, and hobbyists who want to build and deploy simple applications with more capabilities than the Free plan offers.
-   **Features and Benefits**:
    -   More resources compared to the Free plan.
    -   Licenced for personal projects only
    -   Support for small projects that might require additional storage and higher limits on app usage.
    -   Access to more advanced features and integrations.
    -   Suitable for personal use, small non-commercial projects, or learning more advanced features of Anvil.

## [Business Plan](#business-plan)

-   **Who it’s for**: Small to medium-sized businesses or professional developers working on commercial projects that require reliable performance.
-   **Features and Benefits**:
    -   Higher limits on app usage and storage.
    -   Multi-user collaboration, staging environments, and use of private GitHub repositories
    -   Licenced for corporate use
    -   Access to business-specific integrations and tools.
    -   Community support
    -   Ideal for businesses needing to deploy apps for internal use, customer engagement, or as part of their service offerings.

## [Production Plan](#production-plan)

-   **Who it’s for**: Businesses that that require support and high performance from their Anvil applications
-   **Features and Benefits**:
    -   Priority email support from the Anvil team with one-working-day response
    -   Reserved resources for server code and database
    -   Isolated database and direct SQL access to Data Tables
    -   Add more CPU, RAM and storage at AWS cost price
    -   Ideal for businesses serving larger user bases, demanding computations, intensive database access, or who cannot afford to wait for community support

## [Enterprise Plan](#enterprise-plan)

-   **Who it’s for**: Large organizations or enterprises that need full control of their data and deployment, robust scalable solutions and extensive support and customization options.
-   **Features and Benefits**:
    -   Private, single-tenant deployment of the Anvil platform
    -   Available on-premise or managed by our team in the cloud
    -   Highest level of resources and limits.
    -   Dedicated account management and premium support.
    -   Custom solutions and integrations tailored to the enterprise’s specific needs.
    -   Advanced security features, compliance, and SLA (Service Level Agreement) availability.
    -   Best for regulated/sovereign data, large-scale projects, mission-critical applications, and organizations with complex requirements needing full-scale deployment and support.
