# [App Settings](#app-settings)

App Settings lets you edit various settings for your Anvil apps. You can open App Settings from the [Sidebar Menu](/docs/editor#sidebar-menu):

## [](#general)[General](/docs/editor/app-settings/titles-and-logos)

Change the title, description and logo of your app.

## [](#python-versions)[Python versions](/docs/server/python-versions)

Choose the version of Python running on the server.

## [](#dependencies)[Dependencies](/docs/deployment/dependencies#depending-on-other-apps)

Add other apps to use as libraries or configure your app to use as a library.

## [](#data-tables)[Data Tables](/docs/editor/app-settings/data-tables)

Configure settings for Data Tables.

## [](#collaboration)[Collaboration](/docs/editor/app-settings/cloning-and-collaboration)

Work with others by adding collaborators to your app or sharing a clone link.

## [](#embedding)[Embedding](/docs/deployment/embedding-your-app)

Embed your app in an iframe.
