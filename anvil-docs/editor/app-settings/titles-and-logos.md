# [Setting the App Title and Logo](#setting-the-app-title-and-logo)

You can set the title, description and logo of your app in the app’s General Settings, which can be found in the [Sidebar Menu](/docs/editor#sidebar-menu).

The title, logo and description will be displayed when sharing your app on social media. The title is also displayed on the browser tab, and the logo is used for the favicon (the icon that appears in your browser tab). You may need to clear your browser cache to see your new favicon.
