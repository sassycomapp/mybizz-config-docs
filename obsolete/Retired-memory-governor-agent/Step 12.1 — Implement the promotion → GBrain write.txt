First check: the failure mode is exactly right, and it's the safe kind. Second check: remember genuinely accepted 3000 characters and succeeded — remembered as fact #1, no truncation error, no rejection. That's real evidence it isn't hard-limited to short text the way the help text's framing ("one claim per call") had implied.
