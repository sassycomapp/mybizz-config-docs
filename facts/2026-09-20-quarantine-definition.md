# Durable fact — Quarantine definition

Quarantine holds retired-but-valuable documents: content marked obsolete and
removed from the corpus whose information is still needed and still cited.
A quarantined document is no longer part of the live corpus but remains
referenceable. Supersedes the interpretation of quarantine as a mechanism for
blocking cross-project references.
